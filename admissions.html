Admissions & Enrolment Guidelines

A transparent, structured admission guide for students completing Class 10 and aspiring to pursue the Intermediate MPC stream.

Admission Information

Intermediate MPC Program Enrolment

Admission to the two-year Intermediate MPC (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry) stream is open to candidates who have successfully completed Class 10 (Secondary School Certificate / CBSE / ICSE or any recognized equivalent state or national board).

Our admissions team provides comprehensive academic counselling to prospective students and parents, ensuring clarity regarding the rigorous curriculum, laboratory practical commitments, and long-term career aspirations.

Important Policy Note: Eligibility criteria, subject availability, reservation norms, and admission deadlines depend on the applicable state education board and verified institutional guidelines. No cut-offs, fee amounts, or admission guarantees are stated here.

Document Verification

Required Documents Checklist

Candidates are typically required to present self-attested copies along with original certificates for official verification during enrolment:

  • ✓
    Class 10 Marksheet / Passing Certificate:

    Original SSC / CBSE / ICSE or equivalent marks memo.

  • ✓
    Transfer Certificate (TC):

    Issued by the previous secondary school attended, where applicable.

  • ✓
    Conduct / Bonafide Certificate:

    Certification of character and attendance history.

  • ✓
    Identity & Address Proof:

    Valid government ID (such as Aadhaar card) of student and parent.

  • ✓
    Passport-Size Photographs:

    Recent color photographs for student file and identity records.

  • ✓
    Other Board-Mandated Records:

    Migration certificates (for out-of-state boards) and caste/income certificates if seeking reservation as per state norms.
