## 2. Visual Direction & Quality Guidelines for Google Flow

To maintain high aesthetic coherence across the entire website, adhere strictly to these artistic parameters:

- **Art Style:** High-end architectural and editorial documentary photography with natural color grading.
- **Context:** Contemporary Indian Intermediate / Junior College academic setting (Hyderabad / South Indian urban educational campus atmosphere).
- **Lighting:** Soft natural morning or afternoon sunlight, warm diffused indoor ambient lights, non-glaring studio softboxes for faculty portraits.
- **Color Palette:** Warm wood, soft blues, deep navy, clean white architectural accents, subtle academic amber highlights.
- **Subjects:** Focused Indian students (aged 16–18 years) in neat formal student attire (e.g., crisp collared uniforms or semi-formal student dress) and faculty in dignified formal attire (formal shirts, sarees, blazers).
- **Strict Prohibitions:**
  - **NO** embedded text, typography, signs, or watermarks.
  - **NO** college names, fictional academy logos, or branded crests.
  - **NO** unrealistic or sci-fi visual effects.
  - **NO** distorted faces, hands, or surreal AI artifacts.

## 5. Integration Instructions: Replacing Placeholders with Generated Images

Once Google Flow generates your `.webp` image files, follow these simple steps to integrate them:

1. **Save Images:**  
   Place all 60 generated `.webp` files directly into the `assets/images/` directory with their exact filenames (e.g., `assets/images/hero-mpc-college.webp`).
2. **Seamless HTML Integration:**  
   Inside each placeholder wrapper, simply add an `<img>` tag with the corresponding `src` attribute:
   ```html
   <!-- Example in index.html -->
   <div class="img-placeholder-wrapper aspect-16-9">
     <div class="img-placeholder" data-filename="hero-mpc-college.webp">
       <!-- Real image added below -->
       <img src="assets/images/hero-mpc-college.webp" alt="MPC Junior College Campus and Students" loading="lazy">
     </div>
   </div>
   ```
3. **Automatic Display via CSS:**  
   The website stylesheet `assets/css/style.css` is pre-configured with:
   ```css
   .img-placeholder img {
     position: absolute;
     top: 0;
     left: 0;
     width: 100%;
     height: 100%;
     object-fit: cover;
     display: block; /* Overrides default none */
     z-index: 2;
     border-radius: inherit;
   }
   ```
   When the `img` element is present, it will automatically cover the placeholder with perfect aspect ratio and border radius without requiring any structural changes.
